About the Author

Utkarsh, the author of "The Emptiness We Share" and "मैं और मेरे बीच की दूरी", is a contemporary writer and poet known for his deeply introspective storytelling. His works often explore the subtle emotional landscapes of human connection, solitude, and self-discovery.

When he's not writing, Utkarsh enjoys quiet reflective walks, classic jazz, and connecting with readers around the world.
